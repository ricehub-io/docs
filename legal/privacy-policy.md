## Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how RiceHub ("we", "us", "our") collects and processes personal data when you use our website.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently and securely in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).


## Data we collect
When you create an account, we collect:
- Username
- Display name
- Password (stored in hashed form)
- User-uploaded avatar image

For authentication purposes, we store:
- JWT refresh token in a secure HTTP-only cookie


## Automatically processed data
When you access the website, certain technical information such as IP addresses may be processed by:
- Our hosting provider
- Cloudflare

We do not actively store or analyze IP addresses in our application code.


## Purpose of processing
We process your data solely to:
- Create and manage user accounts
- Authenticate users
- Allow users to upload and share Linux "rices"
- Maintain website security

We do not sell or share your personal data for marketing purposes.


## Legal basis
Under GDPR, we process your data based on:
- Performance of a contract (providing your account and platform functionality)
- Legitimate interest (maintaining website security and preventing abuse)


## Data retention
Your account is stored as long as your account exists.
<br/><br/>
You can delete your account in your [account](/account) page.


## Cookies
We use single essential cookie to store a JWT refresh token for authentication purpose.

This cookie is:
- HTTP-only
- Secure
- Necessary for login functionality

No tracking or advertising cookies are used.


## Data security
Password are stored in hashed form.
We implement reasonable technical measures to protect user data from unauthorized access.


## Your rights under GDPR
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Restrict processing
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
